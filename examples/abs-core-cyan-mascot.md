TIMELINE (each panel is one still image; hard cut between panels):
0–3 s — OPENING: character standing in a confident static pose, front three-quarter view. Title pops up: "ABS & CORE".
3–7 s — DEAD BUG SETUP: side view, character lying on back on a mat, arms pointing straight up, hips and knees bent 90 degrees with knees stacked over hips. Text: "DEAD BUG" and cue "KNEES OVER HIPS".
7–11 s — DEAD BUG EXTENDED: hard cut to a new still, side view, one arm extended overhead and the opposite leg extended straight just above the floor, lower back flat on the mat, yellow arrow pointing at the lower back. Cue: "BACK STAYS DOWN".
11–15 s — SIDE PLANK SETUP: front view, character on one side, forearm on the mat with elbow directly under shoulder, legs straight and feet stacked, hips resting on the mat. Text: "SIDE PLANK" and cue "ELBOW UNDER SHOULDER".
15–19 s — SIDE PLANK HOLD: two-panel comparison. Left small panel: hips sagging with a small red X. Right large panel: hips lifted so head, hips and feet form one straight line, yellow arrow along the line. Cue: "HIPS IN LINE".
19–23 s — REVERSE CRUNCH SETUP: side view, character on back, arms flat on the mat beside the body, knees bent 90 degrees lifted over hips. Text: "REVERSE CRUNCH" and cue "ARMS FLAT".
23–27 s — REVERSE CRUNCH CURL: hard cut, side view, hips curled up off the mat with knees drawn toward the chest, arms still flat, small yellow curved arrow at the hips. Cue: "CURL, DON'T SWING".
27–30 s — RECAP: three small panels side by side showing the dead bug, side plank and reverse crunch stills. Large text: "SAVE THIS ROUTINE".
